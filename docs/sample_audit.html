What this report cannot tell you

This is a triage of your export, not a forecast of recovered revenue. The export has no outcomes, so the proposed actions start from priors learned on a synthetic benchmark, not on your customers. They show the decision path and not a tuned recommendation.

Measuring what recovery actually adds needs a randomised holdout: a share of events left untreated, compared against the rest.
